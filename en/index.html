How do I book a tattoo?

It's very simple!

  1. 01

    Send your idea

    Message me through the form or Instagram and tell me about your tattoo idea. Include the approximate size and body placement, and add reference images if you like.

  2. 02

    Discuss the details

    I'll get in touch to discuss the design and give you a quote.

  3. 03

    Reserve your appointment

    Once we've agreed on the price and date, I'll send you the payment details for the deposit to secure your appointment.

  4. 04

    Your studio session

    You review the design, we adjust it to your preferences and transfer it onto your skin. At the end, I apply a dressing and explain the aftercare.
