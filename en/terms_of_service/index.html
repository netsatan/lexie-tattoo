PRISM – Terms and conditions
  • Paying a deposit means accepting these terms and conditions.
  • We only tattoo adults.
  • Booking a session requires a quote and the artist's prior approval of the client's idea.
  • You have three days to pay the deposit and send confirmation as a PDF, screenshot or email from your bank.
  • If the client changes their idea, they must wait again for the artist's approval. The artist may decide not to carry out the new design.
  • In that case, we will try to refer you to another artist who would like to do the design, or return to the original idea approved by the first artist.
  • If no agreement can be reached, the deposit is forfeited as compensation for the artist's time.
  • If the person making the booking stops responding, the appointment is automatically cancelled.
  • If you reschedule at least 14 days before your appointment, we can move the session once without losing the deposit, but no more than 3 months ahead.
  • If you reschedule less than 14 days before your appointment, the deposit is forfeited. Please think carefully before booking.
  • Artists reserve the right to reschedule appointments.
  • If the client does not accept the proposed new date, they may request a refund of the deposit.
  • One touch-up is included in the price within three months of the tattoo session. After three months, touch-ups are charged and quoted individually.
  • Please arrive on time on the day of your session. If you are more than 30 minutes late and have not contacted the studio, the session is cancelled and the deposit forfeited as compensation for the artist's time.
  • Only staff and clients booked for a session may be in the studio. Companions and other third parties cannot attend.
  • Before the session, we provide a consent form to complete and information to read. Completing these documents is necessary to begin the session. They are used to gather appropriate records and assess your health. They are not shared with third parties and are only available in the Prism studio archive.
