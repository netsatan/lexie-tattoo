Portfolio

This gallery features a selection of tattoos I've created. I specialise in fine line, illustrative and graphic styles, creating both delicate, subtle designs and bolder compositions.

My work features botanical motifs, female figures, semi-realism, animals and lettering. I design each tattoo individually, tailoring it to the body, aesthetic and personality of the person who will wear it.

Although most of my clients are women, my tattoos are for everyone. I create both light, minimalist designs and stronger forms that work equally well as tattoos for men.

If you're looking for an artistic tattoo in Warsaw, carefully detailed and tailored to you, take a look through my portfolio.
