How do I book a tattoo?

Message me on Instagram or through the website form. Describe your tattoo idea, approximate size and body placement. I'll use this to suggest an appointment and an initial quote.

lexietattoo Contact form
02

How much does a tattoo cost?

The price mainly depends on the size, detail of the design and body placement. Small, simple tattoos usually start at around PLN 350. A full-day session costs up to PLN 1,800.

I'll give you an exact quote after hearing your idea.

03

Is a deposit required?

Yes, reserving an appointment requires a deposit. It secures your appointment and is deducted from the tattoo price on the day of your visit.

04

What payment methods do you accept?

You can pay in cash, by BLIK or by instant bank transfer. I don't have a card terminal, so card payments aren't possible.

05

What days and hours do you work?

The studio is open Tuesday to Saturday, from 11 am until the session finishes (no later than 8 pm). The latest we can start is 4:30 pm.

06

How long is the wait for an appointment?

From a few days to a month, depending largely on how long your tattoo will take and whether you can come on a weekday. Saturdays book up fastest, so the wait for those can sometimes be longer.

07

Can I reschedule my appointment?

To keep your deposit, you must reschedule at least 7 days before the agreed appointment.

08

What if I'm late for my appointment?

Well, we can't start without you ;) Just let me know you'll be late.
